# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want AI Safe Driver to be a welcoming, useful project for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or ability.

## Our standards

Helpful behavior includes:

- being respectful and specific;
- criticizing ideas and behavior rather than people;
- giving reproducible evidence when reporting a problem;
- accepting corrections and making room for different levels of experience; and
- protecting private information and credentials.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, threats, discriminatory language, personal attacks, deliberate disruption, publishing another person's private information, or sexualized attention.

## Scope

This code applies in repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, and other spaces where someone represents this project.

## Reporting

For a confidential report, open the [conduct contact-request form](https://github.com/ssauma/ai-safe-driver/issues/new?template=conduct_contact.yml). It creates a public issue titled `Private conduct contact requested`; do not include the report details. Provide a contact method you are comfortable publishing, or add one temporarily to your GitHub profile. [@ssauma](https://github.com/ssauma) will use it to continue privately.

The maintainer will review reports fairly, protect the reporter's privacy as far as practical, and respond in proportion to the behavior. Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions, reject participation, or temporarily or permanently ban a contributor when necessary.
